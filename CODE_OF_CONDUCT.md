# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a rigorous and welcoming place for people testing 3D-print
simulation ideas at every experience level.

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-focused. Critique claims and methods,
not people. Share negative results. State uncertainty and conflicts of
interest. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information,
or pressure contributors to overstate their findings.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation when behavior undermines a safe, constructive community.
Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer through their GitHub
profile. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and avoid unnecessary
disclosure.

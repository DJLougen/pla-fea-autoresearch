# Published Bambu PLA Basic DMA reference

## Admitted evidence

- Exact DSC Table 2 values for four nozzle temperatures.
- Twelve Figure 7/Figure 9 storage-modulus points at 30 °C and 1 Hz across four nozzle temperatures and three raster orientations.
- Twelve paired DMA tan-delta peak glass-transition points.
- Independent crossplot rows passing the strict tolerance: 10/12.
- Maximum independent storage crossplot difference: 0.0700 GPa.
- Maximum independent Tg crossplot difference: 0.2560 °C.

## Use restriction

These are plotted summary points, not raw DMA sweeps. Figure 7 and Figure 9 disagree beyond the strict digitization tolerance for some rows; every discrepancy remains flagged in the CSV. The values may be used only for a 30 °C/1 Hz as-printed storage-stiffness sensitivity and a glass-transition guardrail. The paper does not expose enough data to fit damping, a Prony series, creep, or a service-temperature modulus law.
